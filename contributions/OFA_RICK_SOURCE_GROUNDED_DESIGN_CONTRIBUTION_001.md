## 1. The product test is learning capability, not interface polish

The target is not:

```text
beautiful dashboard
+
quiz cards
+
AI chat
+
voice button
```

The target is:

```text
learner encounters something difficult
↓
Rick makes the underlying relationship legible
↓
learner asks / probes / observes
↓
Rick explains in place
↓
Rick yields
↓
learner attempts independently
↓
learner later recognises the relationship without Rick
```

**Keeper:**  
> Ofa Rick succeeds when the learner becomes more capable and less dependent on Rick.

## 4. Voice must be structural

Voice is central to the eventual Living Classroom, but “voice enabled” is not a sufficient quality bar.

The teaching experience should preserve clear Acoustic Turn Ownership:

```text
READY
nothing records or plays automatically

LISTENING
learner deliberately gives Rick the floor

THINKING
system truthfully indicates processing

EXPLAINING
speech and visual emphasis point to the same relationship

YOUR TURN
Rick stops and the learner owns the interaction again
```

Claude should investigate existing Quiet Reader, Maya and Flow mechanisms before creating another speech stack.

A pleasant voice and a complete conversational voice system are different capabilities.

Required distinctions include:

- voice quality;
- microphone truthfulness;
- interruption / barge-in;
- silence;
- replay;
- pronunciation;
- turn ownership;
- visual synchronisation;
- text fallback;
- no-speech operation.

**Keeper:**  
> Rick must know when to speak, when to listen, when to explain, and when to disappear.

---

## 5. Explain in Place is the preferred teaching composition

Avoid forcing the learner to mentally join a paragraph with a separate diagram.

Prefer:

```text
THE OBJECT / RELATIONSHIP ITSELF
↓
Rick points to it
↓
highlight / path / grouping / transformation reveals structure
↓
brief explanation
↓
guidance withdraws
↓
learner attempts
```

Potential reusable visual primitives:

- arrows;
- paths;
- highlights;
- grouping;
- annotations;
- transformations;
- comparison overlays;
- reveal/hide;
- spatial manipulation.

Movement should earn its place by improving understanding.

---

## 6. Teaching mode and assessment mode must remain separate

During **Learn / Practice**, Rick may explain, highlight, replay, adapt and answer.

During **Assess**, Rick must not leak the solution through:

- speech;
- animation;
- visual emphasis;
- timing cues;
- adaptive hints;
- changed task wording.

The assessment state should be quieter and stricter than the teaching state.

**Keeper:**  
> Assistance must disappear when independent competence is being measured.

## 8. One environment, different perceptual states

The Living Classroom should not be one static UI.

The same environment can shift according to the learner's activity:

```text
READING
calm / spacious / low demand

REASONING
visual working surface

PRACTISING
focused / minimal distraction

TALKING TO RICK
conversational teaching surface

ASSESSING
quiet / independent / no hints
```

This is a perceptual-state problem, not merely a navigation problem.

---

## 9. The Yard of Excellence should evaluate one complete encounter

Do not award quality from screenshots.

The eventual acceptance encounter should observe whether the learner can:

1. enter and understand the state;
2. hear Rick;
3. interrupt Rick;
4. see the exact relationship being described;
5. ask a question;
6. receive a better explanation;
7. regain the turn;
8. solve an unfamiliar problem independently;
9. leave;
10. return to the correct useful position.

Also inspect:

- typography;
- keyboard access;
- reduced motion;
- quiet mode;
- truthful microphone state;
- text fallback;
- sensory balance;
- resource consumption;
- preservation of previously solved design problems.

**Keeper:**  
> Excellence is not the presence of components. It is the quality of the relationship between them.

---

## 10. Local-first engineering is part of design quality

Prefer deterministic mechanisms for established operations:

- arithmetic generation;
- answer validation;
- timers;
- spatial transforms;
- visual rendering;
- progress state;
- caching;
- replay;
- accessibility state.

Use intelligence where it adds educational value:

- unexpected misunderstanding;
- alternative explanation;
- unfamiliar question;
- validated new lesson material.

Do not make Claude or a local language model the runtime for every operation.

Do not sacrifice protected services or machine stability to create apparent sophistication.
